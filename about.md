# About Me

Hi, I'm a **Cloud Architect and Red Hat Consultant** specializing in enterprise container platforms, end-to-end IT automation, and modern hybrid cloud infrastructure. 

Since joining Red Hat in 2019, I have helped organizations bridge the gap between legacy infrastructure and cloud-native architectures—architecting, deploying, and hardening resilient platforms powered by **OpenShift** and the **Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform (AAP)**.

---

### What I Do

I work at the intersection of technical architecture, hands-on systems engineering, and client enablement. My core focus is guiding enterprise clients through digital transformation journeys—from initial discovery workshops to production platform deployment, automation governance, and operational handover.

**Key areas of focus:**
* **Enterprise Ansible Automation:** Architecting and implementing Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform (AAP) across hybrid environments, defining automation governance, building custom Execution Environments (EE), and orchestrating multi-tier workflow job templates.
* **Event-Driven & Day-2 Operations:** Implementing Event-Driven Ansible (EDA) for self-healing infrastructure, automated remediation, proactive patch management, and continuous compliance enforcement across sprawling server fleets.
* **Enterprise Kubernetes & OpenShift:** Multi-cluster architectures, platform security, Day-2 lifecycle management, and Single Node OpenShift (SNO) implementations.
* **Modern Virtualization:** Workload modernization and VM migration using OpenShift Virtualization (KubeVirt), integrating Ansible to automate VM provisioning and lifecycle hooks.
* **Infrastructure as Code & GitOps:** Automated bare-metal and cloud provisioning using Ansible, Terraform, and GitOps (ArgoCD) to drive consistent, declarative state across clusters and host OS layers.

---

### Industry Track Record

Over the past 5+ years, I have architected and delivered solutions for heavily regulated enterprise environments across North America:

* **Financial Services & Insurance:** Automated configuration compliance, security auditing, and zero-downtime platform maintenance using AAP, reducing manual provisioning times from weeks to minutes.
* **Government & Healthcare:** Built secure, isolated container platforms and automated bare-metal RHEL provisioning, meeting stringent federal security baselines and data sovereignty mandates.
* **Cross-Functional Consulting:** Partnered closely with engineering, security, and executive teams to scale automation adoption across organizational silos through reusable roles, collections, and COE best practices.
