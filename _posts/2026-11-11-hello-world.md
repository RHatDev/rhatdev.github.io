Writing technical articles helps clarify your thoughts and creates a searchable knowledge base for your future self. 

### Why You Should Start Writing

1. **Better Understanding**: If you can't explain a bug or concept simply, you don't understand it well enough.
2. **Personal Portfolio**: Your posts showcase your problem-solving process to peers and employers.
3. **Helping the Community**: Solutions to niche issues can save hours for other developers.
